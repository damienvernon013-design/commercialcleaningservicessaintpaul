What Does a Commercial Office Cleaning Service Actually Include?

What's standard, what's an add-on, and what to ask your provider to confirm.

Blog

Not all commercial cleaning services are the same, and what's included in a quote varies between providers. Before signing a contract, it helps to know exactly what's in scope.

Standard Inclusions

Vacuuming. All carpeted areas, including under desks and in corners.

Hard floor mopping. Tile, vinyl, and hardwood swept and mopped.

Trash removal. All bins emptied and relined.

Restroom cleaning. Toilets, sinks, mirrors, and floors scrubbed and disinfected; consumables restocked.

Kitchen and breakroom. Counters, sink, and appliance exteriors wiped down.

Surface dusting. Desks, shelves, and window ledges.

Glass and mirrors. Interior glass and partitions.

Common Add-Ons

Carpet extraction, floor stripping and waxing, exterior window washing, high dusting, and post-construction cleanup are usually priced separately from nightly service.

What to Ask Your Provider

Ask the provider to walk you through every task they will and won't perform, and request a written scope of work rather than a verbal agreement. This gives you a clear basis for evaluating their performance.

Customization Is Standard

A good commercial cleaning company will tailor service to your space — a server room that should never be entered, a conference room used only weekly, or specific kitchen equipment should all be documented in the scope.
