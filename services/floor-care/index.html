Floor Care

Hard floors are the highest-visible surface in most commercial facilities and the most expensive to repair when neglected. Saint Paul's climate adds an annual variable that doesn't apply everywhere: winter salt, tracked in from November through March, attacks finish coatings and concrete sealers at a rate most maintenance schedules don't account for.

Our floor care programs for Saint Paul buildings are built around the actual conditions your floors face — not a generic strip-and-wax cycle copied from a warmer market.

Salt Season Planning for Saint Paul Facilities

Road and sidewalk salt is the most common cause of premature finish failure in Twin Cities commercial buildings. Calcium chloride and sodium chloride both penetrate finish coatings on contact, and standard mops spread rather than remove them. We plan a pre-winter scrub-and-recoat for most Saint Paul floors in October, and a post-winter interim clean in April when the last salt load has been tracked in and dried. Buildings that follow this schedule typically go two full years between full strip-and-wax cycles.

How Floor Care is Priced

Floor care in Saint Paul is priced per square foot, per service. Strip-and-wax rates depend on the number of coats, drying time required, and whether furniture must be moved. We quote floor care separately from nightly cleaning — some customers use us for floors only; most add floor care as a scheduled supplement to their nightly program. Rates are included in your written quote.

Before the first service: We walk the floors with you and note existing condition — scratches, worn finish zones, embedded grit. That baseline protects both parties and sets realistic expectations for what a first-service outcome looks like.
