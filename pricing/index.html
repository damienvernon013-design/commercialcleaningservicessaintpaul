Commercial Cleaning Services Pricing in Saint Paul, MN

How commercial cleaning is priced in the Twin Cities market, and what drives the numbers up or down.

Pricing

Commercial cleaning pricing in Saint Paul is quoted per visit, per square foot, or as a monthly flat rate depending on the service type and frequency. The ranges below reflect current market conditions in the Saint Paul metro. Your actual quote will depend on your facility's specifics.

How Nightly Commercial Cleaning is Priced

Nightly cleaning in Saint Paul is typically quoted on a per-visit or monthly basis. The per-square-foot rate for a five-night-per-week program generally runs between $0.08 and $0.18 per sq ft per month for standard office space, depending on the factors below. A 10,000 sq ft office building on a nightly schedule in Saint Paul should expect a monthly quote in the range of $800–$1,800 per month.

That range is wide because the following factors all affect price in this market:

FactorEffect on rate
Higher restroom count per sq ftRate increases — restrooms are time-intensive per sq ft
Open plan vs. heavy office partitionOpen plan is faster to clean, lower rate
Medical or lab spaces requiring disinfection protocolsRate increases — protocol adds time per visit
Late-night or weekend access onlyRate increases — shift differential applies
High foot traffic (retail, clinic lobbies)Rate increases — soil load is higher
Multi-floor buildings with freight elevator accessRate increases — equipment transport adds time

Floor Care Pricing

Floor care is priced per square foot per service. Strip-and-wax for VCT in Saint Paul currently runs $0.15–$0.30 per sq ft depending on the number of finish coats and whether furniture must be moved. Scrub-and-recoat runs $0.08–$0.15 per sq ft. Carpet extraction is typically quoted at $0.12–$0.22 per sq ft.

Floor care is priced separately from nightly cleaning and scheduled independently. Most customers combine both under a single contract with separate line items.
