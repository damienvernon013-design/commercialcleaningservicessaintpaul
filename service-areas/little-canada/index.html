Service Area: Little Canada, MN

Little Canada is a small commercial suburb north of Saint Paul along Rice Street and I-35E. We have been servicing commercial cleaning customers in this community as part of our Saint Paul metro operations for 22 years. The drive time from our Saint Paul base to Little Canada is approximately 18 minutes from our Saint Paul base via Rice Street or I-35E.

What Commercial Facilities in Little Canada Require

Little Canada's commercial real estate is concentrated along Rice Street and the I-35E service roads, with a mix of professional services, medical offices, and light industrial tenants in single and two-story buildings built largely between 1975 and 2000.

The city hosts several medical and dental practices along the Rice Street corridor that draw patients from the surrounding northern suburbs, creating a steady demand for clinical-grade cleaning protocols in the area's healthcare-adjacent commercial buildings.

Little Canada's proximity to Maplewood and Roseville means commercial cleaning contracts in the area can be efficiently combined with nearby routes, which we pass along as consistent service without inflated travel surcharges.
