How Often Should a Commercial Office Be Professionally Cleaned?

Getting frequency right means you're not overpaying for service you don't need — or undercleaning until it shows.

Blog

This is one of the most common questions from businesses shopping for commercial cleaning. The right answer depends on a few factors, and getting it right avoids both overpaying and letting things slide.

The General Rule

For most office environments, two to three cleaning visits per week strikes the right balance between hygiene, cost, and necessity. High-traffic offices, client-facing spaces, and food-prep areas typically need nightly service.

Factors That Determine Frequency

Employee count. More people means more mess and more high-touch surfaces. Smaller offices can often get by with one to two visits weekly; larger open-plan offices usually need nightly service.

Client traffic. If clients or customers regularly visit your space, cleanliness ties directly to your brand — nightly service is usually worth it.

Facility type. Medical, dental, and food-service spaces generally require daily attention regardless of headcount.

Layout. Open-plan offices with shared surfaces accumulate grime faster than private offices with fewer shared touchpoints.

Frequency by Facility Type

Small professional offices typically run one to two visits per week. Mid-size offices run two to three. Larger open-plan offices and medical or dental suites usually run nightly.

Don't Skip Periodic Deep Cleans

Regardless of your regular frequency, schedule periodic deep cleans for carpets and hard floors. Most providers offer these as scheduled add-ons at a favorable rate for existing customers.
