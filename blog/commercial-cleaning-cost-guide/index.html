Commercial Cleaning Costs in Saint Paul: A Pricing Breakdown

Real Saint Paul metro pricing ranges, the factors that move your quote, and how to evaluate what you're offered.

Blog

If you've called around for commercial cleaning quotes in Saint Paul and gotten wildly different numbers back, you're not alone. Pricing varies by a wide margin depending on a handful of factors — and understanding them puts you in a stronger position when comparing bids.

What's the Average Cost in Saint Paul?

Nightly commercial cleaning in the Saint Paul metro runs approximately $0.08 to $0.18 per square foot per month for standard office space on a five-night program. For an 8,000 sq ft professional building cleaned nightly, that works out to roughly $800–$1,100 per month. Medical suites, dental offices, and restroom-heavy buildings typically fall at the higher end due to disinfection requirements and fixture count.

Key Factors That Affect Your Quote

Square footage. Larger buildings cost more in total but often less per square foot as fixed setup costs spread across more space.

Frequency. Nightly service costs more overall than weekly, but the per-visit rate is usually lower because crews are already on a routine schedule.

Facility type. Medical and dental suites require EPA-registered disinfectants and documentation, which adds to the rate. Light industrial and warehouse space with a small office component is usually priced lower per square foot.

Restroom count. Restrooms are the most time-intensive space to clean per square foot in any commercial building.

Access hours. Buildings that can't be entered until late evening require a shift differential that adds to the base rate.

One-Time vs. Recurring Service

One-time deep cleans are priced separately from recurring contracts, and recurring service is almost always more cost-effective per visit over the long term.

What's Usually Included

Standard nightly cleaning quotes in Saint Paul typically cover vacuuming and mopping, restroom sanitation, trash removal, surface wiping, and breakroom cleaning. Floor care — strip-and-wax, scrub-and-recoat, carpet extraction — is quoted separately, since it runs on its own schedule and requires different equipment.

Get a Written Quote Before Deciding

Pricing varies enough between providers that a written, itemized quote is the only reliable way to compare. A quote that comes in significantly below these ranges is usually cutting scope somewhere.
