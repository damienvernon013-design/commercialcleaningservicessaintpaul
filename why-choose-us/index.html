Why Choose Us

Most commercial cleaning companies in Saint Paul have been operating for under five years. The industry has high contractor turnover, inconsistent crew quality, and a tendency to win contracts on price and then cut corners on scope. We have been cleaning Saint Paul commercial facilities for 22 years. That history is not a marketing line — it is a track record you can check.

22 Years in the Saint Paul Market

We have been servicing commercial buildings in Saint Paul since the early 2000s. We have cleaned through the 2008 recession, through the COVID shutdowns of 2020, and through every winter season this city produces. The crews who started with us are largely still here. The customers who came to us in year one are largely still with us.

That kind of continuity doesn't happen without consistent performance. It also produces something most newer contractors can't offer: genuine knowledge of how Saint Paul's specific business environment — the Midway District, the downtown Lowertown revival, the healthcare corridor along University Avenue — actually runs.

Written Documentation at Every Stage

Most cleaning companies operate on verbal agreements. You say you want the lobby mopped nightly; they say they'll do it; there is no document. When something is missed, there's no record of what was agreed, and the dispute is word against word.

We use written scopes for every engagement. The scope exists before the first visit, and any changes to it are made in writing. This protects both parties and makes quality control straightforward: the checklist is the checklist.

Price Transparency

We publish pricing ranges on this site. Most competitors won't give a number until you've signed an NDA and sat through a sales presentation. We think buyers in the Saint Paul commercial market deserve to know roughly what to expect before investing time in a walkthrough.

The ranges on our pricing page are accurate for current Saint Paul market conditions. Your specific quote may fall above or below depending on your facility, but it will be in the same order of magnitude — not a bait-and-switch.

Insured and Bonded

We carry general liability insurance, workers' compensation coverage, and a commercial cleaning bond. The certificate of insurance is included with every written quote. If something is damaged or goes missing in your facility, it is addressed through our coverage, not yours.
