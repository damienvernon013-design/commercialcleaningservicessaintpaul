Nightly Commercial Cleaning

Most commercial facilities in Saint Paul run a nightly cleaning schedule — five nights per week, after the last employee leaves. The reason is simple: daily foot traffic deposits soil faster than any less-frequent schedule can address. Letting it accumulate is what leads to deep cleaning bills, premature floor refinishing, and facilities that look older than they are.

What a Nightly Visit Covers

Our nightly commercial cleaning in Saint Paul starts from a written scope agreed before the first visit. The scope is not a general checklist — it maps tasks to specific rooms, surfaces, and frequencies based on your actual building. Standard scope items for most Saint Paul commercial properties include:

  • Trash removal and liner replacement in all office and common area receptacles
  • High-touch surface disinfection: desks, countertops, light switches, door handles, elevator buttons
  • Restroom disinfection, supply restocking, floor mopping
  • Vacuuming of carpeted areas and matting
  • Hard floor dust mopping and damp mopping where scheduled
  • Kitchen and break room wipe-down: counters, sink, exterior of appliances
  • Glass and partition wipe-down at entry points

Tasks outside this core — periodic deep cleans, floor stripping and waxing, window washing — are scheduled separately and quoted separately. The nightly scope covers what needs to happen every night, reliably.

Facilities We Service Nightly in Saint Paul

Our nightly commercial cleaning crews currently work in office buildings throughout downtown Saint Paul and the surrounding commercial corridors, including properties along Kellogg Boulevard, University Avenue, Energy Park Drive, and Bandana Square. We also service light industrial and flex-space buildings in the Midway district and along the I-94 corridor.

Typical building sizes on our Saint Paul nightly schedule range from 3,000 sq ft professional suites to 35,000 sq ft multi-floor office buildings. The scope and pricing differ; the standard of execution does not.

Crew Consistency and Supervision

Nightly cleaning fails most often from crew turnover — a building gets a trained crew for four weeks, then a rotating cast of unfamiliar faces who don't know where the janitor's closet is or which conference rooms skip vacuuming on Fridays. We address this by assigning a dedicated crew to each building and keeping a crew supervisor accountable for quality on each shift.

If something is missed, you contact us directly and we correct it the next night. In 22 years, we have not had a building that needed more than one correction before the issue stopped recurring.

Starting a Nightly Service

The typical sequence for a new nightly cleaning contract in Saint Paul: phone or form inquiry, walkthrough of your facility, written scope and quote returned within one business day, scope review call if needed, start date confirmed. Most buildings are cleaned for the first time within two weeks of initial contact.

No lock-in required. We quote nightly cleaning on month-to-month terms. If the service isn't what you expected after the first 30 days, you're free to cancel. In practice, most of our Saint Paul nightly contracts have been running for multiple years.
