Service Radius

Our 25-mile service radius from Saint Paul covers the following types of communities in the metro area: inner-ring suburbs with dense commercial corridors (Roseville, Maplewood), residential suburbs with significant office and retail stock (Mendota Heights, Falcon Heights), and older industrial communities with warehouse and light manufacturing facilities (South Saint Paul, West Saint Paul). The crew drive time from our Saint Paul base to any of these communities is under 30 minutes under normal conditions.

If your facility is outside the listed communities but within the 25-mile radius, contact us — we may be able to service it depending on crew routing.
