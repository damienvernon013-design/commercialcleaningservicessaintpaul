Understanding Your Commercial Cleaning Contract

What to check before signing, section by section.

Blog

A commercial cleaning contract defines your service relationship. It's worth more than a quick skim, since the details determine what happens if something goes wrong.

Scope of Work

The most important section. It should itemize every cleaning task, the areas covered, and the frequency for each. Vague phrases like "general cleaning" should prompt a request for a detailed task list before signing.

Service Frequency and Schedule

The contract should state clearly how often service occurs and on which days.

Pricing and Payment Terms

Confirm whether pricing is monthly, per-visit, or per-square-foot, and when invoices are due.

Contract Duration and Renewal

Know the term length and any notice window required to avoid auto-renewal.

Termination Clause

Understand what it costs to exit early, and whether a mutual termination option exists if service quality falls short.

Complaint and Remedy Process

How do you report a problem, and what remedy is offered — a re-clean, a credit?

Insurance and Liability

The contract should confirm the provider carries appropriate liability coverage, with a certificate of insurance available on request.

Everything Is Negotiable

Ask for a trial period and clear performance expectations tied to your complaint resolution process before signing a longer-term agreement.
